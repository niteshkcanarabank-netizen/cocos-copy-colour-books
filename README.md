# 🦁 Coco's Copy-Colour Books

40 copy-colour activity books for kids aged 2–12, arranged in 4 age-based series. Every page pairs a big bold outline with a small coloured reference, so little artists learn by copying colours.

## 🌐 Website

Open **index.html** (or enable GitHub Pages) to browse all 40 books with covers and free PDF downloads.

## 📚 The Series

| Series | Ages | Books |
|---|---|---|
| 🦁 Coco's First Colours | Ages 2–4 | My First Pets, Sea Animals, Farm Animals, Fruits, Vegetables, Things That Move, Wild Animals, Birds, Flowers, Toys |
| 🎈 Coco's Play & Learn | Ages 4–6 | Dino Friends, ABC Fun, 123 Numbers, Community Helpers, Outer Space, Unicorns & Princesses, Jungle Animals, Ocean World, Construction Vehicles, Festivals of India |
| 🚀 Coco's Adventures | Ages 6–8 | Insects & Bugs, Sports Stars, Robots, Superheroes, Pirates & Treasure, Knights, Dragons & Castles, Mermaids & Ocean Magic, Emergency Heroes, Zoo Animals, Monster Trucks & Big Wheels |
| 🌟 Coco's Creative Studio | Ages 8–12 | Mandalas, Kawaii Doodles, Anime Chibi Style, Affirmations, Zendoodle Patterns, Detailed Wildlife, Dragons & Fantasy Beasts, Galaxy Space, Indian Mythology, Fashion Design Studio |

## 🎨 Format

- 16 copy-colour pages per book (17 pages incl. cover), 8.5 × 11"
- Bold outlines + small colour reference on every page
- Meet **Coco** 🦁, the lion cub artist who guides every book
